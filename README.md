# Publifix

[![Run Backend Tests](https://github.com/pranav162074/Publifix/actions/workflows/test.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/pranav162074/Publifix/actions/workflows/test.yml)

Public spaces break down, potholes go unfilled, streetlights stay dark, garbage piles up, drains overflow and most of the time there's no simple way for a citizen to report it and actually know what happens next.

Publifix is a platform that lets people report damaged or neglected public infrastructure in their area, track the status of their complaints over time, and stay informed as issues move from reported to resolved. It's built to close the gap between citizens noticing a problem and the right people knowing about it.

The goal is simple: make it easy to speak up about what's broken around you, and easy to see that someone's actually listening.

## What it does

- Citizens can sign up and log in with an email/password or their Google account
- Report an issue with a title, description, category, and a photo
- Your device's location is attached automatically (with permission), so every complaint can be pinned on a map
- Track the status of everything you've reported from a personal dashboard
- A profile page shows your account details and a breakdown of your complaints by status
- Get an email the moment an admin updates one of your complaints
- Admins get a dedicated panel to review every complaint in the system and move it through its lifecycle: pending, in-review, in-progress, resolved, or rejected
- A public map shows every reported issue in the area, open to anyone, logged in or not

## Under the hood

The project is built end to end as a full-stack application, covering authentication, file uploads, third-party API integrations, automated testing, and a CI/CD pipeline that runs the test suite on every change before it's allowed into the main branch.
