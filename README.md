Publifix

Public spaces break down — potholes go unfilled, streetlights stay dark, garbage piles up, drains overflow — and most of the time there's no simple way for a citizen to report it and actually know what happens next.

Publifix is a platform that lets people report damaged or neglected public infrastructure in their area, track the status of their complaints over time, and stay informed as issues move from reported to resolved. It's built to close the gap between citizens noticing a problem and the right people knowing about it.

The goal is simple: make it easy to speak up about what's broken around you, and easy to see that someone's actually listening.
